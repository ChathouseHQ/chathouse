---
title: Two-factor authentication
description: Secure your Chathouse account with TOTP-based two-factor authentication.
---

Chathouse supports two-factor authentication (2FA) using Time-based One-Time Passwords (TOTP). This adds an extra layer of security by requiring a 6-digit code from your authenticator app in addition to your password when logging in.

## Setting up 2FA

Navigate to **Settings > Security** and click **Set up two-factor authentication**.

<img alt="2FA setup" src="/docs/img/usage/2fa-setup.png" />

<Steps>
<Step>

### Scan the QR code

Open your authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy, 1Password, or any TOTP-compatible app) and scan the QR code displayed on screen.

If you can't scan the QR code, click the **manual entry key** link to reveal a text code you can enter into your authenticator app manually.

</Step>
<Step>

### Verify the code

Enter the 6-digit code displayed in your authenticator app into the verification field.

</Step>
<Step>

### Confirm with your password

Enter your account password to confirm. Click **Enable** to activate 2FA.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn">
  Make sure to save a backup of your authenticator app's data. If you lose access to your
  authenticator app, you won't be able to log in.
</Callout>

## Logging in with 2FA

When 2FA is enabled, the login flow becomes a two-step process:

1. Enter your email and password as usual
2. Enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app

<img alt="2FA login prompt" src="/docs/img/usage/2fa-login.png" />

## Disabling 2FA

To disable 2FA, go to **Settings > Security** and expand the **Disable two-factor authentication** section. You'll need to provide:

- The current 6-digit code from your authenticator app
- Your account password

Click **Disable 2FA** to turn it off. You can re-enable it at any time by going through the setup process again.
