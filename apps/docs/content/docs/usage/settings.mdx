The account and preferences settings let you personalize your Chathouse experience. Navigate to **Settings > Account** for profile settings or **Settings > Preferences** for application behavior.

## Profile

### Avatar

Upload a profile picture by clicking on your avatar in **Settings > Account**. Supported formats are JPEG, PNG, GIF, and WebP, with a maximum size of 5 MB.

<img alt="Account settings" src="/docs/img/usage/account-settings.png" />

If you don't upload an avatar, Chathouse generates a unique one for you automatically based on your name.

To remove your avatar, click the **Remove** button below it and confirm.

### Display name

Your display name appears in the chat greeting and on shared conversations. Enter a name in the display name field — it saves automatically when you click away.

## Changing your email

To update your email address, enter the new email in the email field under **Settings > Account**. You'll need to enter your current password to confirm the change.

## Changing your password

Under **Settings > Account**, fill in the password change form:

1. Enter your **current password**
2. Enter your **new password** (minimum 8 characters)
3. **Confirm** the new password
4. Click **Save**

<Callout type="info">
  If you've forgotten your current password, use the **Forgot password** link on the login page to
  receive a password reset email (requires [SMTP to be
  configured](/selfhosting/configuring#email-smtp)).
</Callout>

## Preferences

Navigate to **Settings > Preferences** to configure how Chathouse behaves.

<img alt="Preferences settings" src="/docs/img/usage/preferences.png" />

### System prompt

Set a default system prompt that's prepended to every new conversation. This is useful for setting a consistent tone or giving the AI standing instructions (e.g., "Always respond concisely" or "You are a helpful coding assistant").

The field saves automatically when you click away.

### Chat history

Toggle whether your conversations are saved. When disabled, new chats won't be persisted after you leave them (similar to temporary chats, but applied globally).

### Chat retention

Set how many days your chats are retained before being automatically cleaned up. The default is **90 days**, and you can set it anywhere from 1 to 365 days.

### Title generation

Choose how chat titles are generated:

| Option               | Description                                                                                                                     |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **AI-generated**     | Uses a lightweight AI model to generate a descriptive title based on the conversation. This uses a small amount of API credits. |
| **First characters** | Uses the first few characters of your first message as the title. Free, but less descriptive.                                   |

<Callout type="info">
  AI-generated titles require at least one [connected provider](/usage/connections). If no providers
  are connected, this option will be disabled.
</Callout>

## Admin settings

If you're the instance administrator (the first user who registered), you'll see an additional **Admin Settings** section on the Preferences page.

### Registration

Toggle whether new users can register on your Chathouse instance. After your account is created, registration is automatically closed. You can re-open it here if you want to invite others to use your instance.
